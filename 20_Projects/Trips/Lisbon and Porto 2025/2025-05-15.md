# 2025-05-15

Gaia lodges, Dom Luís bridge upper deck, and a tasting without hard-sell packages.

## Day timeline

- 09:00 — Breakfast
- 10:00 — Cross Dom Luís I upper deck
- 11:30 — Port tasting in Gaia
- 13:30 — Lunch in Gaia
- 16:00 — Optional cable car skipped; walk back
- 19:30 — Dinner in Ribeira

## Logistics

- Upper deck walk is free and better for photos than the cable car.
- One lodge tasting is enough.
- Watch pickpockets in crowded bridge photo spots.

## Places
