    # Temple etiquette and crowds

    Early starts beat tour groups at Fushimi Inari and Arashiyama.

    ## Key points

    - Shoulders/knees respectful at sacred sites.
- Fushimi before 08:00.
- Bamboo grove early or late — avoid mid-morning peak.

    ## Details

    One heavy temple day + one soft café day keeps energy sustainable.
