# 2024-09-12

Transfer day: morning Freccia to Florence, hotel check-in, sunset at Piazzale Michelangelo.

## Day timeline

- 08:30 — Breakfast and bags to Termini
- 09:50 — Freccia Roma Termini → Firenze SMN
- 11:30 — Arrive Florence; walk/taxi to Hotel Davanzati
- 13:00 — Lunch near hotel
- 17:30 — Walk to Piazzale Michelangelo
- 19:30 — Dinner in Oltrarno

## Logistics

- Freccia is reserved seating — be on platform early.
- SMN to Hotel Davanzati is walkable with light bags; taxi if heavy.
- Sunset climb to Piazzale is steep but short.

## Places

- [[Florence]] — arrival and sunset overlook *(half day)*
- [[Rome]] — departure city *(quick stop morning only)*

## Food

- **Breakfast:** Hotel / Termini café
- **Lunch:** Station or centro panini
- **Dinner:** Oltrarno trattoria
- **Groceries / snacks:** Ekiben-style train snacks from Termini
