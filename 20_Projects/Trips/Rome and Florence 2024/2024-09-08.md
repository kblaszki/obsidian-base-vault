# 2024-09-08

Arrival day in Rome: settle near Termini, then a gentle evening loop through the historic centre to shake off travel fatigue.

## Day timeline

- 11:40 — Land FCO; passport control and bags
- 12:30 — Leonardo Express to Termini
- 13:30 — Check in Hotel Artemide; drop luggage
- 16:00 — Walk Trevi → Pantheon area
- 19:30 — Dinner near Trevi
- 21:30 — Early night for jet lag

## Logistics

- FCO → Termini via Leonardo Express (buy before boarding).
- Hotel is a short walk from Termini; taxis optional if tired.
- Evening walk is flat and crowded — keep valuables front-facing.

## Places

- [[Rome]] — city base and orientation walk *(half day soft)*
- [[Pantheon]] — exterior and piazza at dusk *(quick stop)*

## Food

- **Breakfast:** Airport or skipped if late
- **Lunch:** Light panini near Termini after check-in
- **Dinner:** Trapizzino near Trevi
- **Groceries / snacks:** Water and fruit from a Termini-area supermarket for the room

### Costs

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Leonardo Express | €14 | Airport rail |
| Dinner | €28 | Trapizzino + drinks |

## Pack today

- **Documents:** Passport / EU ID; hotel booking
- **Clothes:** Comfortable walking shoes; light layer for evening
- **Cash / cards:** Card fine; small cash for tip jars
- **Gear:** Daypack only; leave big suitcase in room

Keep day one light. Caput Mundi overload can wait until tomorrow.
